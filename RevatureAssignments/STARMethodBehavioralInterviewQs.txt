STAR Method Behavioral Interview Questions
By Patrick Guinn

1. Tell me about a time where something did not go as planned and how you handled the situation?
2. Tell me about a time when you had a disagreement with someone and how you handled the situation?
3. Tell me about a time when you had to adapt to a new challenge.
4. Have you ever had to develop a new skill on the fly? Tell me about your approach to the learning process.
5. Give an example of a time when you failed to hit your goals. How did you respond?
6. Give an example of a time when you performed well under pressure.
7. Tell me about a time when you had to help a teammate who was struggling.
8. Tell me about a time you felt completely overwhelmed by a technical task. What did you do?
9. Give an example of a time you had to complete a task with very little guidance or direction.
10. Give me an example of a time when you took initiative.
